Zero Data Collection Guarantee

Privacy Policy for Omarchy Watch Face

Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Application: Omarchy Watch Face (org.omarchy.watchface)

1. Overview

Omarchy Watch Face is built as a pure, privacy-first, declarative watch face for Wear OS smartwatches. We believe your wrist data is strictly yours. This watch face does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal, biometric, or usage data.

2. No Code Execution & Pure Watch Face Format

This watch face is implemented exclusively using Google’s modern Watch Face Format (WFF v2) with hasCode="false".

  • There is no executable Kotlin, Java, or C/C++ application code running in the background.
  • There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no crash reporting telemetry.
  • The watch face does not request or possess the android.permission.INTERNET permission, meaning it is technically incapable of transmitting any data over the network.

3. Sensor & Health Data (Complications)

When complications such as battery status, step count, or heart rate are displayed:

  • Data is provided strictly by the Wear OS operating system (such as Samsung Health or Google Health Services) through standard on-device system complication providers.
  • All calculations and visual renderings occur locally in hardware on your watch's display engine.
  • No sensor readings are retained, logged, or sent anywhere.

4. Third-Party Services

Omarchy Watch Face does not use or integrate any third-party services, cloud APIs, tracking pixels, or external databases.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
